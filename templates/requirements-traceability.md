# Requirements traceability matrix

| Requirement ID | Type | Requirement | Source | Priority/status | Acceptance evidence | Architecture element(s) | Interface/data contract | Test(s) | Stage/ADR | Open issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Types: `FR`, `NFR`, `CONSTRAINT`, `SECURITY`, `DATA`, `ML`, `OPERATIONS`, `GOVERNANCE`.

Every required-now component must trace back to at least one requirement. Every high-priority requirement must trace forward to a design element and verification method.
